## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, high-precision industrial aesthetic rooted directly in technical textile, supply-chain logistics, and B2B manufacturing. Drawing cues from the logo's deep midnight canvas, engineering cyan, and safety amber, the brand projects authority, structural resilience, and digital clarity.

The style converges **Corporate / Modern** with subtle **Industrial Technicalism**:
- **Confidence & Groundedness:** Uncompromising structural balance, deep navy grounding layers, and strict modular geometry convey institutional stability.
- **Engineered Accents:** Vivid technical cyan acts as the guiding operational beacon (navigation, primary actions, confirmed states), while safety amber provides decisive industrial alerts, active highlights, and conversion triggers.
- **Textile & Machine Motifs:** Tactical touches inspired by weave patterns, barcode identification strips, micro-dashes, and monospaced technical metadata punctuate content containers without degrading into skeuomorphism.

## Colors

The palette is extracted directly from the core chromatic identity of the insignia, structured for high contrast, strict readability, and industrial situational awareness.

- **Primary (`#009FE3` / Technical Cyan):** Derived from the fluid letterform 'S'. Communicates digital precision, technical connectivity, and active interactive elements. Used for primary actionable buttons, focus states, verified badges, and progression bars.
- **Secondary (`#E67E00` / Safety Amber):** Extracted from the angular terminal 'L'. Represents dynamic industrial energy, procurement alerts, primary conversion highlights, quotes, and critical production indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#5A6D8C` / Slate Weave):** Derived from the subdued barcode dashes and textile weave tracks. Powers low-prominence tags, grid lines, structural dividers, inactive track lines, and secondary analytical charts.
- **Neutral (`#0B132B` / Midnight Canvas):** The foundational dark enclosure. Extends down to `#060A17` for deep canvas wells and steps up to `#131F3F` for raised container cards and data panels.
- **Surface Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`):** Extracted from the bold central 'T'. Provides stark typographic hierarchy, uncompromised legible readout against midnight backgrounds, and crisp icon silhouettes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an engineered dual-track system:

- **Display & Section Headers (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers clean geometry with industrial quirks that parallel modern logistics and structural mechanics. Headlines are kept tight and deliberate.
- **Reading & Data Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides pristine legibility in enterprise tables, supply specifications, and dense transactional dashboards without creating optical friction.
- **Operational Data & Micro-Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Directly implements the barcode/swatch aesthetic from the brand mark. Monospaced rendering handles part numbers, SKU codes, logistics statuses, coordinates, batch weights, and micro-metric telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an uncompromising 12-column responsive fluid grid structured for technical density and data-intensive manufacturing workflows.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates multi-tier ERP navigation, catalog filtering, and multi-metric logistics monitoring side-by-side.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Modules collapse into structured 2-up and 4-up cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Data grids reflow into vertically stacked inventory modules.
- **Rhythm & Alignment:** All components and vertical gaps conform to an 8px base grid rhythm (4px micro-increment for compact input rows and data tables). Structural panels feature dashed alignment dividers mimicking technical weave patterns.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through tonal layering and technical low-contrast illumination, avoiding muddy ambient drop-shadows.

1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deepest midnight navy (`#060A17`), representing unlit production bedrock.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Primary surface blue-slate (`#0E1733`), framed with a 1px border of `rgba(90, 109, 140, 0.22)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Elevated Drawers):** Elevated navy (`#16234D`), accented with a subtle 1px border highlight of `rgba(0, 159, 227, 0.4)` and a soft 12px technical glow (`rgba(0, 159, 227, 0.08)`).
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Menus):** Top-tier container (`#1A2B5E`), supported by a directional industrial edge shadow (`0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`) with an outer outline of `rgba(230, 126, 0, 0.35)` when context requires warning or confirmation priority.
5. **Textile Accent Lines:** Key structural boundaries feature a dashed hairline stroke (`1px dashed rgba(90, 109, 140, 0.35)`) directly mirroring the stitch guides from the official identity.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** shape language to balance industrial machine precision with accessible modern software usability.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** Shaped with a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving the feel of milled panels and precision-cut components.
- **Cards, Enclosures & Data Tables (`rounded-lg`):** Configured at `0.5rem` (8px), mirroring the subtle curved container boundaries of the brand badge.
- **Modals & Flyouts (`rounded-xl`):** Configured at `0.75rem` (12px) to softly distinguish floating utility layers from base layout grids.
- **Barcode & Swatch Chips:** Pure rectilinear tags (`0px` or strict pill geometry depending on categorical tier) to maintain barcode-inspired visual rhythm.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cyan Engine):** Background `#009FE3`, label in `#060A17` bold typography, `0.25rem` radius. On hover, transitions to `#29B4F0` with a subtle technical cyan back-glow (`0 0 14px rgba(0, 159, 227, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary / Direct Conversion (Safety Amber):** Background `#E67E00`, label in `#FFFFFF`, with high-contrast tactical visibility for actions like "Request RFQ" or "Confirm Batch Dispatch".
- **Outline / Ghost (Industrial Frame):** Surface transparent, border `1px solid rgba(90, 109, 140, 0.4)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover reveals `rgba(0, 159, 227, 0.1)` fill and `#009FE3` border.

### Chips & Swatch Badges
- Direct nods to the bottom barcode motif in the brand identity.
- Categorical and status tags feature monospace (`JetBrains Mono`) uppercase text accompanied by a square color-coded 4px swatch dot.
- High-priority order statuses use `#E67E00` background tints; inventory and verification statuses leverage `#009FE3`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Fields:** Dark container background (`#0B132B`), 1px outline in `#2A3A5E`, text in `#FFFFFF`, placeholder in `#5A6D8C`.
- **Focus State:** 1px ring in `#009FE3` with 0-offset focus glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square and circular forms with `#009FE3` fill on selection, featuring crisp white checkmarks.

### Cards & Industrial Modules
- Midnight panel background (`#0E1733`), bordered with low-opacity slate (`#1E2C52`).
- Optional header accent: 2px horizontal top edge indicator strip in technical cyan or safety amber to designate item classification.
- Optional footer divider: 1px dashed stitch border (`rgba(90, 109, 140, 0.3)`) separating data parameters from operational actions.

### Telemetry & Technical Tables
- Compact row height (`44px`), alternating row zebra-striping with `#0E1733` and `#0B132B`.
- Numeric data, weights, SKU matrices, and shipping containers set in monospaced typography with right-aligned tabular formatting.
